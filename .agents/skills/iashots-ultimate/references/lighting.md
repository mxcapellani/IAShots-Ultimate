# Layered light reconstruction

Describe observed key direction/softness, ambient fill, back/rim separation, background illumination and visible practicals. Add only layers supported by evidence or clearly proposed as a reconstruction technique.

Relate shadows and reflections to the same sources. Apparent colour temperature is approximate; do not recover exact Kelvin or equipment models from appearance. Metal, fabric and glass respond differently.

Use camera-left/camera-right consistently. Do not silently reverse light during a translation or field split. A brighter background does not imply equal facial exposure. Make the highest-value visible light relationships more detailed than minor decorative effects.
