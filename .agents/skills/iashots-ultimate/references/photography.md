# Photographic composition

Preserve crop, orientation, camera height, subject placement, negative space and foreground/midground/background relationships. Perspective is governed by camera position; a focal-length equivalent is a creative suggestion, not observed EXIF.

Describe garments by type, cut, colour, material, closure, folds and accessories actually visible. Do not replace detailed clothing with generic elegance. Separate garment appearance from the source person's physical traits.

Scale, contact shadows, occlusion and depth must agree. Do not request a fully dissolved background while requiring distant architecture to remain sharply readable. Avoid plastic texture, sharpening halos and gratuitous optical effects.
